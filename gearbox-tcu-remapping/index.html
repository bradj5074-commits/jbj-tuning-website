What TCU tuning is

A TCU controls shift behaviour in many modern automatic and dual-clutch transmissions. Where supported, calibration can alter permitted torque handling, shift response and other software-controlled behaviour.

Vehicle compatibility

TCU tuning is highly vehicle-specific. We confirm the exact model, gearbox and software support before quoting or carrying out work.

Engine and gearbox packages

Where both ECU and TCU tuning are supported, we can discuss a combined package so the engine and transmission calibrations are suitable for one another.
