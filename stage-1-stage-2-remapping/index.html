Stage 1 remapping

Stage 1 is generally aimed at compatible vehicles running standard hardware. The objective is a balanced calibration for improved response and torque while staying within sensible limits for the vehicle.

Stage 2 remapping

Stage 2 may be appropriate where a vehicle has suitable supporting hardware and the ECU can be calibrated accordingly. Compatibility is checked first, and not every vehicle requires or benefits from Stage 2.

Before we tune
