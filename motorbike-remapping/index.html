Motorbike Remapping

For compatible motorcycles, ECU tuning can refine software-controlled response and performance characteristics. JBJ offers motorcycle remapping support across Chester, Wrexham, Flintshire and North Wales.

Supported motorcycles

Motorbike ECU support depends on the make, model, year and ECU type. Send us the registration or full bike details so we can check compatibility.

Calibration goals

Where tuning is supported, the calibration is chosen around the bike's setup and intended use rather than applying a one-size-fits-all approach.

Mobile availability

Availability depends on the ECU and required access method, but many supported jobs can be completed as part of our mobile service.
