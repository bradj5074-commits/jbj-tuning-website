Modern diesel emissions systems can generate warning lights, reduced power and fault codes when something goes wrong. JBJ provides mobile diagnostics and lawful solutions for supported DPF, EGR and AdBlue system issues.

DPF-related faults

DPF warnings can have several causes, including sensor faults, failed regeneration or underlying engine issues. Correct diagnosis is important before deciding on a repair strategy.

EGR and AdBlue faults

EGR and AdBlue systems can also trigger diagnostic codes and drivability problems. We can investigate supported systems and discuss appropriate repair or lawful-use options.

Road-use compliance

Emissions-system modification can be restricted for road-going vehicles. We do not present illegal road-use defeat work as a normal repair; any service offered is subject to the vehicle, its intended use and applicable law.
